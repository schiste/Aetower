Aetower

Developer Preview · macOS

Local observability for operators and AI agents.

Aetower inspects process pressure, wakeups, memory, energy, code-signing, and local agent impact — and nothing leaves your Mac unless you export it.

What it inspects

Process pressure

CPU, threads, and a friction score that ranks what's actually straining the machine.

Wakeups & energy

Idle wakeups and energy impact translated to battery, cost, and carbon.

Memory & leaks

Footprint over time with vmmap-backed region breakdowns for any process.

Code-signing

Cached signature verdicts that flag unsigned and ad-hoc binaries running across your fleet.

Startup & persistence

Launch agents, daemons, login items, and cron — each with its signing status.

Agent impact

Per-agent run timelines, budgets, and an MCP server for your own tooling.

By default

Local-first

Runtime history and diagnostics stay on your Mac unless you explicitly export them.

Conservative

Endpoint Security, telemetry, and automatic MCP registration are all off until you opt in.

Installer first

The PKG is the supported path; DMG, Homebrew, ZIP, and source are there if you prefer them.
